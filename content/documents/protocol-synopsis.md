## 1. Objectives and endpoints

| Type | Objective | Endpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | To compare the efficacy of CBX-417 + docetaxel with placebo + docetaxel with respect to progression-free survival | **PFS**: time from randomization to first documented progression per RECIST 1.1 (investigator assessment) or death from any cause, whichever occurs first |
| Key secondary | To compare overall survival between arms | **OS**: time from randomization to death from any cause |
| Secondary | To compare objective response | **Confirmed ORR**: proportion of subjects with a confirmed complete response (CR) or partial response (PR) per RECIST 1.1 |
| Secondary | To describe the durability of response | **DoR**: time from first documented response (later confirmed) to progression or death, in confirmed responders |
| Secondary | To characterize safety and tolerability | Adverse events (NCI CTCAE v5.0), laboratory values, vital signs, ECG, dose modifications and exposure |
| Exploratory | To explore consistency of treatment effect across subgroups and biomarker categories | PFS and OS by prespecified subgroups, including PD-L1 tumor proportion score category |

## 2. Study design

Randomized, double-blind (for CBX-417 vs placebo), placebo-controlled, multicenter, parallel-group study. Subjects are randomized **1:1** to:

- **Arm A**: CBX-417 200 mg QD + docetaxel 75 mg/m² q3w
- **Arm B**: matching placebo QD + docetaxel 75 mg/m² q3w

Randomization is performed centrally through an interactive response system (IxRS) using permuted blocks within strata, stratified by:

1. **ECOG performance status** at screening: 0 vs 1
2. **Prior anti-PD-(L)1 therapy**: Yes vs No

The IxRS assigns blinded kit numbers; the block size is documented in the randomization specification and is not disclosed to sites. Docetaxel is administered open-label in both arms. The study is event-driven: the final PFS analysis is performed after **331 PFS events** have been observed.

## 3. Eligibility (abbreviated)

**Key inclusion criteria**

1. Age ≥ 18 years at informed consent.
2. Histologically or cytologically confirmed advanced (stage IIIB not amenable to curative therapy) or metastatic (stage IV) NSCLC.
3. Radiologic progression during or after 1–2 prior lines of systemic therapy for advanced disease, including a platinum-containing doublet.
4. At least one measurable lesion per RECIST 1.1 on imaging obtained within 28 days before randomization.
5. ECOG performance status 0 or 1.
6. Adequate organ function within 14 days before randomization (including absolute neutrophil count ≥ 1.5 × 10⁹/L, platelets ≥ 100 × 10⁹/L, hemoglobin ≥ 90 g/L, total bilirubin ≤ 1.5 × ULN, ALT and AST ≤ 3 × ULN (≤ 5 × ULN with liver metastases), creatinine clearance ≥ 50 mL/min).
7. Written informed consent.

**Key exclusion criteria**

1. Known actionable *EGFR* mutation or *ALK* rearrangement.
2. Prior treatment with docetaxel for advanced disease, or any prior CBX-417.
3. Untreated or symptomatic central nervous system metastases (treated, stable, asymptomatic brain metastases off steroids ≥ 14 days are permitted).
4. Clinically significant cardiovascular disease within 6 months, or QTcF > 470 ms at screening.
5. Active infection requiring systemic therapy, or any condition that in the investigator's judgment would compromise participation.
6. Pregnancy or breastfeeding; refusal to use highly effective contraception where applicable.

## 4. Treatment and dose modification

- **CBX-417 / placebo**: 200 mg (4 × 50 mg tablets) QD, continuously, in 21-day cycles, until progression, unacceptable toxicity, withdrawal, or physician decision. Dose reductions: 200 → 150 → 100 mg. Interruptions of up to 21 consecutive days are permitted; longer interruptions require sponsor discussion.
- **Docetaxel**: 75 mg/m² IV over 1 hour on Day 1 of each cycle. Dose (mg) = 75 × BSA, with BSA by the Mosteller formula, BSA = √(height [cm] × weight [kg] / 3600). One reduction to 60 mg/m² is permitted, typically after febrile neutropenia or grade ≥ 3 neutropenia. Docetaxel may be discontinued while CBX-417/placebo continues.
- **End of treatment** is the last dose of any study drug.

## 6. Statistical considerations

**Sample size.** 331 PFS events provide 90% power to detect a hazard ratio of 0.70 at a two-sided significance level of 0.05, using Schoenfeld's formula

d = 4 (z₀.₉₇₅ + z₀.₉₀)² / (ln HR)² = 4 (1.9600 + 1.2816)² / (ln 0.70)² = 330.4 → **331 events**.

With the planned accrual of 420 subjects over approximately 20 months and an assumed control median PFS of about 4.2 months, 331 events are expected approximately 34 months after the first subject is randomized.

**Interim analysis.** One interim analysis of PFS is planned after approximately 66% of the planned events (≈ 219 events), performed by an independent statistical group for an Independent Data Monitoring Committee (IDMC). Type I error is controlled with a Lan-DeMets alpha-spending function of the O'Brien-Fleming type. The sponsor study team remains blinded.

**Primary analysis.** PFS is compared between arms with a log-rank test stratified by the randomization stratification factors as recorded in IxRS. The hazard ratio and its 95% CI are estimated from a stratified Cox proportional hazards model. Kaplan-Meier estimates, including medians with 95% CIs, are presented by arm. Details, including censoring rules, sensitivity analyses and the testing hierarchy for OS, are specified in the statistical analysis plan.

**Analysis populations.** Efficacy analyses use the ITT population (all randomized subjects, analyzed as randomized). Safety analyses use the safety population (all subjects who received at least one dose of any study drug, analyzed by treatment actually received).

## 7. Safety reporting

Adverse events are graded with NCI CTCAE v5.0 and coded with MedDRA. Serious adverse events are reported to the sponsor's pharmacovigilance function within 24 hours of site awareness, and expedited reports are submitted to regulatory authorities as required. Disease progression is recorded as an efficacy outcome and is not reported as an adverse event unless the investigator considers the clinical manifestation an adverse event.

## 8. Ethics and conduct

The study is conducted in accordance with the protocol, the principles of the Declaration of Helsinki, ICH Good Clinical Practice and applicable regulations. The protocol and informed consent documents are approved by an institutional review board or independent ethics committee before enrollment at each site.
